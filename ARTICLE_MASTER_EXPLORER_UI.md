# Article Master Explorer UI

The Articles / Items workspace now uses the catalogue hierarchy as its navigation model.

## Layout

- Left: Major Group -> Item Group -> Article explorer.
- Right: selected article master record.

## Article information

The selected article view includes:

- SKU and catalogue path
- on-hand quantity and reorder level
- base stock unit
- reference cost (read-only on this page)
- stock status
- business classification
- batch and expiry controls
- maximum stock level
- active supplier quotation coverage
- configured UOM conversions
- current store balances where available

Supplier prices remain maintained only through Supplier Quotations. This page does not introduce a separate price-edit path.
