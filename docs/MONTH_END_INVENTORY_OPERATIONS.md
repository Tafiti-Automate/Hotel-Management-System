# Month-end stocktake operational release candidate

The client workflow has a dedicated Cost Control and Finance workspace, official memo print preview, property/location register, blind count sheets sorted by group/article, saved physical quantities, post-submission value differences and investigation, explicit refresh/recount, independent Finance approval, atomic booking through the existing stock-count ledger, zero consumption-only department inventory, and a monthly fiscal close blocker.

Important implementation limitations: Physical snapshots and submissions must occur on the cutoff business date with an open fiscal period ending on that date. Later investigation, independent approval and booking are permitted only with a complete cutoff-dated submission, no post-cutoff stock movements, unchanged balances/batches and no closed subsequent fiscal period. Ledger business_date preserves the cutoff for valuation, GL previews and stock-card reporting while created_at preserves actual posting time. Arbitrary historical count reconstruction is deliberately prohibited. Date-sensitive integrations still require staging verification. Known batches are physically counted separately and reconciled atomically with aggregate stock variance booking. Unrecorded batch provenance, pre-existing batch/stock drift or unresolved quarantined batch stock still block booking; no batches or expiry dates are invented. Department classification is manually confirmed because the department master is global. Unexpected articles without existing balances require inventory reconciliation before counting; a changed store roster needs controlled remediation. Official memo print and in-app notification to active department heads and Finance in the property are included. External email delivery is not configured. The variance report includes individual closing values. A booked monthly inventory value reconciliation now compares previous booked closing values with valued movements and current booked closing values, breaks down movement categories and lists direct departmental expenses separately. It flags missing opening/valuation evidence rather than inventing a zero. It is not a certified food/beverage COGS or GL report. Full financial/ledger and UI acceptance remains required. Do not deploy until migration checks, regression tests, cross-property/role tests and witnessed client/Finance sign-off pass.

Late stock movements and roster changes after booking block period close; a controlled reconciliation/rebooking procedure for those exceptions remains to be designed and witnessed. No historical stock or financial posting is permitted without additional evidence.
