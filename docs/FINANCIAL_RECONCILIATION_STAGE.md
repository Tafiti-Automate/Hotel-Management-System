# Financial reconciliation and indirect cash flow
- Indirect report starts with posted period profit, removes profit/loss associated with investing and financing cash journals, and bridges operating and noncash journals through their noncash balance movements at account level. Direct and indirect operating cash and closing cash differences are shown. This is a provisional bridge, not an independently certified IAS 7 presentation; working-capital/noncash classification and the client's exact report format remain to be reviewed. Cash classifications remain manual, and operational sources are not yet auto-posted.
- Account reconciliation records evidence references and manually supplied normal balances (debit positive for assets, credit positive for liabilities/equity) for cash, bank, inventory, supplier, customer, payroll and other balance-sheet accounts. It calculates GL cutoff balance, difference and a ledger fingerprint. A separate Financial Manager/General Manager can confirm an exact match; backdated ledger changes make it stale. It does not import bank statements, match individual transactions, verify external evidence automatically or auto-adjust balances.
- No existing records are changed, no journals are auto-posted and no sample financial figures are imported. Asset additions/disposals/revaluations, reversals, close integration, exact templates, source posting, POS and PMS remain outstanding.
