# Department Approval History

Department Heads now have four focused Store Request views:

- Pending — requests awaiting a decision.
- Approved — requests they approved, including requests now at Stores, issue, or completion stages.
- Rejected — rejected department requests.
- All requests — the department's non-draft request history.

After approval, the request leaves Pending but remains available under Approved and All requests. Selecting a historical request opens its item details, approval information, timeline, and current workflow status. Historical records are read-only.
